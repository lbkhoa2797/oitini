The dominant exchange mechanism is **90° superexchange** via the bridging halide X. According to GKA rules, this near-90° Cr–X–Cr geometry favors **ferromagnetic** coupling — consistent with the observation of ferromagnetism in CrBr₃ and CrI₃.
